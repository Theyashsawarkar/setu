v0.2.02026-11-07release ↗

A new look, and pairing you can see.

  • Connected devices are cards at the top, two per row, each with a battery ring: green, yellow under 20% ("charge soon"), red under 10% ("plug it in"). Devices UPower reports several batteries for (earbuds left, right, case) show each one.
  • Headphones and speakers say what they're doing: "Playing · high quality" or "Calls · with mic", and which one is the sound output. The preview has a Music / Calls switch, and Ctrl+O makes the selected device the sound output (needs pactl).
  • Paired devices say when they were last connected, and "Out of range" when they couldn't be reached.
  • Nearby devices are sorted closest first, with signal bars and a word (Close, Near, Far). Unnamed beacons hide behind Ctrl+U ("Ctrl+U shows 6 unnamed").
  • The header says the name other devices see ("Visible as ...") and, in yellow, "Looking for devices" while it searches.
  • Pairing is a step of its own: the 6-digit code shown big to compare with the device, "They match" (Enter) or Cancel (Esc), the steps Found, Confirm code, Connect, and a countdown. Before, a device that asked for a code made pairing hang until it timed out.
  • Errors say why in plain words ("Device didn't answer: put it in pairing mode"), on the row and in the preview.
  • Remove asks first: Ctrl+Backspace twice (Esc keeps the device).
  • No bluetoothctl, no Bluetooth service, no adapter or Bluetooth off: a clear message with how to fix it, instead of the tool being left out.
  • Needs Vyom 0.6.
  • Every corner is rounded: the website's callouts.
  • A logo and a shloka at the top of the README and the website: every Vayu tool has them now.
  • Screenshots in the README and on the website.

v0.1.02026-10-19release ↗

  • First release, as its own repository: bluetooth. Replaces bluetooth-picker.py in Vayu.
  • Your devices, connected first, each with its battery level and the right icon (headphones, mouse, keyboard, phone…).
  • Discovers nearby devices while the panel is open; nameless beacons are left out.
  • Enter connects or disconnects; a nearby device pairs, trusts and connects in one go.
  • Remove (unpair), and a Bluetooth on/off switch.
