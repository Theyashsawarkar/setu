Changelog

Every change to setu, newest first. Releases are git tags (all tags, GitHub releases); the source of this page is CHANGELOG.md.

Unreleased

  • Hidden, it stops searching (Vyom 0.13, ADR 17): Vyom now keeps panels open per workspace, hiding one when you leave its workspace or another panel takes the centre. While it's hidden the Bluetooth scans stop (a pairing under way goes on); when it shows again, as you left it, it searches again (parked() / unparked()).
  • Website: pranali joins the family list in the footer; the site builder passes ruff.
  • CI installs PyGObject with GTK 4, cairo and gtk4-layer-shell from apt (and runs the system Python) so the tests that import GTK run there too; they failed on GitHub with No module named 'gi' since they were added.

v0.6.02026-11-27Release notes

  • The list is there when the panel opens: the adapter, every device and their batteries come from Vyom's live state (BlueZ's and UPower's own signals keep it current), not a bluetoothctl info per device and upower -d on every refresh; a device found, connected or charging shows by itself while the panel shows (at most one redraw a second while a scan pours in). With an older Vyom it asks bluetoothctl, as before.
  • The buds' scan can no longer run on after the panel closes: closing it while BlueZ was still starting the scan left it on (and kept the Bluetooth radio busy).

v0.5.12026-11-26Release notes

  • The case keeps its battery. Earbuds only report the case while the buds are in it (or it's open); with the buds in your ears the case said "—". setu now remembers the case's last level per device and shows it a step quieter with its age ("Case · 2 hours"), charging only while that's under an hour old. The tooltip says why.
  • The device's icon is drawn centred on its own shape, not on the font's box: the headphones sat a few pixels off in their glass tile.

v0.5.02026-11-26Release notes

  • A new look: the picked device, big. No more cards and rings: connected devices are the list's first rows, each with its battery at the right (the lower bud for earbuds) and a peach speaker mark on the sound output. The device you pick shows on the right as a showcase: its icon in glass, its name and state, the batteries as one strip of numbers (Left · Right · Case for earbuds), the audio mode, round buttons with their keys under them, and one quiet line of details. "Pair only" has its own icon.
  • Works every time, no reboot. Connecting tries again (up to three times) when BlueZ fails in a way a second try fixes, and counts a device that connected despite an error as connected; a device that's away gets one more try, a refusal none. A chip that stopped answering (after sleep, mostly) shows "Bluetooth isn't answering" with Reset (Enter): it restarts the controller with an rfkill block and unblock, no root and no reboot. The NotReady error names Space p p, not the old Ctrl+B.
  • Turning Bluetooth on lifts a soft block. When Bluetooth was blocked by rfkill (or switched off somewhere else), Turn on only told you to run rfkill unblock bluetooth; now it does that itself and turns the adapter on, and says so if a switch or the airplane-mode key is what's blocking it. The Turn on button shows Enter, not the old Ctrl+B.
  • The panel no longer opens wider than the others when earbuds report every battery (it now fits the card: "L 82% · R 76% · Case 34%"); the playing line uses the card's room before it cuts off; the Nearby hint names the real key to show unnamed devices (Space u h, not Ctrl+U).
  • New screenshots in the Spatial look, taken with made-up demo data (WebP, sharp on high-density screens).
  • A calmer Bluetooth panel. The header says only what's unusual ("Visible as …" when others can find this computer; "Searching" while it looks), not "Named … · hidden" every time. Device cards have a lit edge, and the one you're on is brighter glass with a light rim instead of a thick white frame. The device's details are one quiet list, and its buttons sit side by side (Disconnect and Remove, Connect and Remove, Pair & connect and Pair only).
  • The website, closer to Vayu's own: the tool's screenshot blurred behind the page, a centred hero with the logo and the verse, the page you're on as the white pill, glass "On this page" and footer panels, releases as cards. No glow behind the logo, no coloured line beside the verse.
  • Spatial look: rounder, borderless glass cards, gauges and pairing card; an unknown earbud gauge is drawn dashed.
  • The website has Vayu's Spatial look: graphite glass, a floating navigation bar, near-white for links and the main button, and the tool's own colour only as its signature (the logo's glow, the shloka's line, the release dots). No more all-caps labels.

v0.4.02026-11-09Release notes

  • Earbuds' left, right and case batteries, each on its own, from Google Fast Pair: the buds announce them (with a charging flag) in their Bluetooth LE advertisements, which setu reads with a quiet LE scan of its own while the panel is open (listening only; the scan stops when it closes). Three gauges side by side in the preview (red at 20% or less, yellow at 40% or less, a green bolt while charging, — for a part that didn't say), L 100 · R 90 and a ring each on the card. BlueZ's single level stays for devices that don't say more. Matched to the connected device by name; the advertiser's own entry no longer shows in Nearby.
  • tests/: the Fast Pair parser (a real OnePlus Nord Buds 3 Pro advertisement, charging, the discoverable form, short and malformed data), run in CI. SETU_FASTPAIR_FAKE (a JSON of name → service data) feeds it for screenshots and tests.

v0.3.02026-11-09Release notes

  • Vim keys (Vyom 0.7): setu opens in NORMAL mode, where letters are keys and typing changes nothing; / (or i) types a filter, Esc goes back. Space is the leader, with a which-key strip; ? lists every key and Space Space is a palette of every action.
  • New keys: Space p p turns Bluetooth on or off, Space a a / a p pair and connect / pair only, Space c c / c d connect / disconnect, Space v o makes it the sound output, Space v m / v c switch headphones to music / calls (that switch was mouse-only), Space u h shows unnamed devices, Space y a copies the address, r rescans.
  • Remove asks with one question: dd (or Space d d) asks "Remove …?", y removes and any other key keeps it, instead of pressing twice.
  • Typing is safe: Ctrl+Backspace deletes a word in the filter again (it removed a device); it now says remove moved to dd. Ctrl+U scrolls half a page up, as in every vim tool (unnamed devices: Space u h). Ctrl+B, Ctrl+O and Ctrl+P still work.
  • While a pairing runs, every key but Enter and Esc is ignored.
  • Keys are data (setu/vim.py); scripts/keybinds.py writes the # keybind: lines and the README table from it (--check in CI).
  • Needs Vyom 0.7.0.

v0.2.12026-11-08Release notes

  • The Bluetooth switch is Vyom's ui.switch() (Vyom 0.6.2), with its own fix for GTK's baselines warning, instead of setu's copy of it.

v0.2.02026-11-07Release notes

A new look, and pairing you can see.

  • Connected devices are cards at the top, two per row, each with a battery ring: green, yellow under 20% ("charge soon"), red under 10% ("plug it in"). Devices UPower reports several batteries for (earbuds left, right, case) show each one.
  • Headphones and speakers say what they're doing: "Playing · high quality" or "Calls · with mic", and which one is the sound output. The preview has a Music / Calls switch, and Ctrl+O makes the selected device the sound output (needs pactl).
  • Paired devices say when they were last connected, and "Out of range" when they couldn't be reached.
  • Nearby devices are sorted closest first, with signal bars and a word (Close, Near, Far). Unnamed beacons hide behind Ctrl+U ("Ctrl+U shows 6 unnamed").
  • The header says the name other devices see ("Visible as ...") and, in yellow, "Looking for devices" while it searches.
  • Pairing is a step of its own: the 6-digit code shown big to compare with the device, "They match" (Enter) or Cancel (Esc), the steps Found, Confirm code, Connect, and a countdown. Before, a device that asked for a code made pairing hang until it timed out.
  • Errors say why in plain words ("Device didn't answer: put it in pairing mode"), on the row and in the preview.
  • Remove asks first: Ctrl+Backspace twice (Esc keeps the device).
  • No bluetoothctl, no Bluetooth service, no adapter or Bluetooth off: a clear message with how to fix it, instead of the tool being left out.
  • Needs Vyom 0.6.
  • Every corner is rounded: the website's callouts.
  • A logo and a shloka at the top of the README and the website: every Vayu tool has them now.
  • Screenshots in the README and on the website.

v0.1.02026-10-19Release notes
